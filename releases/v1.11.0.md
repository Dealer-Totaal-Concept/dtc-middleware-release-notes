## Highlights

This release turns the CK implementation into a substantially broader end-to-end platform. It adds proposal-level business rules and automated proposal sorting, expands the unfinished-application and dealer-portal flows, introduces kredietbeschermer orchestration, and materially extends Findio/CA AutoFinance, HCF, Lender & Spender, DEFAM, and Qander support.

## Known limitations

- Qander supporting-proof mapping is fail-closed and currently only maps the documented identity proof family automatically; other proof IDs require explicit numeric input until the full provider list is mapped.
- HCF still has provider-contract gaps for rent allowance, fuel type, and payout destination, and its API does not yet expose a confirmed HK/PL wire discriminator.
- Lender & Spender BKR/redemption completeness and annual-figure mapping remain dependent on additional source data.
- Kredietbeschermer still has a documented Dazure partial-success/CRM trace risk; the open revised process is not part of this snapshot.
